1. Introduction

Welcome to MathCraft: Visual Geometry (package ID: com.royals.mathcraft). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user privacy and data handling.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

MathCraft operates with a strict offline-first, zero-data-collection architecture:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, request, or store your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or contacts.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or create an account to use any feature of MathCraft.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not track or access your GPS coordinates or location data.
  • No Advertising or Device IDs: We do not collect advertising identifiers (AAID, IDFA) or device serial numbers.

3. Local Storage on Device

All geometric constructions, mathematical calculations, and puzzle progressions happen entirely on your local device. Minimal configuration (such as your chosen Dark/Light theme and audio mute setting) is stored locally on your device via localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your application or browser cache.

4. Audio & Microphone Permissions

MathCraft uses the standard Web Audio API solely to generate local procedural sound effects (tactile mechanical clicks on grid snap and harmonic frequency sonification of geometric curves). MathCraft DOES NOT access your microphone, record audio, or collect voice data.

  • No Personal Profiling: The license check only confirms order validity; it does not collect personal identifiers or track user behavior.
  • No Advertisements or Analytics: MathCraft is 100% ad-free, contains no third-party ad networks, and never sells or transfers user data.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

MathCraft: Visual Geometry is an educational tool safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. Because we collect zero personal data, MathCraft fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Security

Since all operations and calculations remain locally on your device without transmitting data over external servers, your mathematical work remains private and secure under your device's native security sandbox.
