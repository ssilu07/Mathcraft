## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
MathCraft: Visual Geometry operates strictly on a **privacy-by-design, local-first** architecture:
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect, request, or store your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or biometric data.
- **No Account Registration:** The application requires no login, sign-up, or user account to access its complete features.
- **No Location Tracking:** We do not track, collect, or monitor your GPS, IP address, or approximate location data.
- **No Device Identifiers or Fingerprinting:** We do not collect advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or device serial numbers.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage & Preferences
All mathematical constructions, geometric shapes, proof states, and puzzle completions take place strictly on your local device.
- **Local Storage (`localStorage`):** The app stores minimal user configuration data locally on your device, specifically:
  - Your selected display theme (`dark` or `light` mode).
  - Your audio preference (`muted` or `active`).
- **Data Retention & Deletion:** This configuration data remains entirely on your local device. You can erase this data at any time by clearing your device's browser/application cache or uninstalling the application.

## 4. Hardware & Audio Permissions
- **Web Audio API (Procedural Harmonic Synthesis):** MathCraft uses the Web Audio API solely to generate local real-time mathematical soundscapes and tactile feedback (such as sine wave frequencies from the unit circle and mechanical clicks on grid snap).
- **No Audio Recording:** MathCraft does **NOT** access your microphone, record ambient audio, or collect any voice data.
